Delete your SmartBuild account

This page explains how to delete your SmartBuild account (package com.smartbuild.app) and the data linked to it.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open SmartBuild and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top right of the Home screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then confirm.

Option 2: by email (without the app)

Send an email to YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL@example.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject "SmartBuild account deletion". We will delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and password)
  • All saved learning progress for Modules 0–4

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. No data is kept after the account is deleted. Progress cached on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
