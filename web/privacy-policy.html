SmartBuild Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

SmartBuild (package com.smartbuild.app) is an educational app for Computer Systems Servicing (CSS) NC II. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

DataWhy
Email address To create your account, sign you in, and send account emails (confirmation and password reset).
Password To sign you in. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
Learning progress (module progress percentage, guided and assessment completion) To save your progress to your account so it is available when you sign in again.

SmartBuild does not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or payment information. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics.

How the data is stored

  • Account and progress data are stored with Supabase, our backend provider.
  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Each student can only read and change their own progress.
  • A copy of your progress is also cached on your phone so the app loads faster.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except with Supabase to operate the app, or when required by law.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open your profile menu and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all saved progress. You can also request deletion without the app: see Delete your SmartBuild account.

Children

SmartBuild is intended for senior high school, TVET, and college learners. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
